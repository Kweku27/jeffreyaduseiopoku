Currently residing in Berlin, I'm a designer, sustainable development practitioner, and consultant with over 8 years of experience leading and contributing to programmatic work in the area of sustainable development.

I founded Sustainability Week Accra, a week-long program promoting climate and sustainability actions and lifestyles within universities, supported by the Swiss Agency for Development Cooperation and Sustainability Week International.

I kickstarted the SchoolFood4Future Advocacy Campaign, a school meals initiative aimed at improving school meals access for underprivileged children within Ghana, supported by the UMI FUND.

I co-founded Graceham Academy with my Mother in 2021, a co-educational Montessori-inspired school in Ghana offering quality education to children from Crèche to Grade 9.

I am building Earthhaus, a community garden project bringing nature, environment, and food systems closer to children and youth in Ghana.

I am a skilled event, forum, workshop, and conference facilitator and moderator. My highlights include hosting the World Future Policy in Bonn and Geneva, as well as sessions at the World Food Forum in Rome and the Global Citizen Youth Workshop in Paris. Contact me for your next session.

I consider myself an Environmental Economist, but with a primary interest in food systems, social entrepreneurship, and design.
